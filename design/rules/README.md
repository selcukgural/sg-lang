# Decisions by topic

The rules of the language, each with the reason it was chosen, one page for each part of it. On
every page the rows keep the order they were decided in; a row that changed says when and why. The
principle they all follow, and the idea of the language, are on [Decisions and open questions](../04-decisions-and-open-questions.md).
A new decision goes at the end of the table of its page, with an `<a id>` before its topic, and into
the list from A to Z below.
